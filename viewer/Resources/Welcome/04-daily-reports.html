The power move is not just generating a report. It is saving reports as a durable local timeline you can browse later.

Design idea

Give each daily report a hero with the date, a compact signal shelf, a table of project status, and a final "next actions" block. It should feel less like a log and more like mission control.

Use with automation

For recurring reports, keep two kinds of files: stable files that HTTMELY always opens, such as today.html and weekly.html, and dated archive copies that preserve history. An AI assistant or local script can refresh the stable pages, then save a dated copy into archive/.

The bundled release materials include a report-automation skill for this pattern. It is useful when you want clear rules for what gets overwritten, what gets archived, and how private source paths stay out of the final report pages.
