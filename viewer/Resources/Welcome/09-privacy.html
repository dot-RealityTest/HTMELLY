HTTMELY
Privacy
Local First No account, no upload workflow

Your pages stay where you put them.

HTTMELY opens folders you choose and displays files from disk. It is designed to make local pages useful without turning them into a cloud product.

Privacy Notes

What stays local

  • Your HTML and Markdown files remain in their original folders.
  • Saved Places are stored in local macOS user defaults.
  • Window, sidebar, selected place, and contents-panel state are stored locally.
  • Temporary reader files, when needed, are written to the local cache.
