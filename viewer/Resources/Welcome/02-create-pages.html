Create Collections
Make Pages Write, export, generate, archive

A collection is just a folder with good pages inside.

You can write pages by hand, export them from another app, generate them with an AI helper, or save websites for offline reading. HTTMELY does not care where the pages came from. It just makes them readable.

Folder Shape

Use a clear first page.

My Collection/
  index.html
  overview.md
  notes/
    research.html
    checklist.md
  images/
    diagram.png

Design for reading.

  • Use one strong title and semantic headings.
  • Keep local images next to the page or in an assets folder.
  • Use relative links between pages.
  • Avoid remote scripts, login-only assets, trackers, and hosted CSS.
  • Make filenames readable, because filenames become part of the browsing experience.

The best HTTMELY folders feel like a private website you can keep, inspect, export, and archive.
