Why It Works

HTTMELY is not just a reports app.

It is a local-first reading surface for anything that can become HTML or Markdown. The app gives generated files a native place to live, so your work can become a browsable library instead of a loose pile of exports.

The workflow is simple: make pages, put them in a folder, add the folder as a Place, and read them like a small private website.
