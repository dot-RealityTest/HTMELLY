About Native, local, quiet

HTTMELY is a small native home for local knowledge.

It exists because useful work often ends as files: reports, drafts, audits, saved pages, project notes, and generated documentation. Those files deserve a better reader than Finder preview.

Principles

What the app cares about

  • Local first. The source of truth is a folder on your Mac.
  • Readable by default. Pages get a calm native shell and a focused sidebar.
  • Portable output. HTML and Markdown remain normal files.
  • No account required. The app does not need a service to be useful.

HTTMELY is not trying to own your content. It is trying to make your local content easier to use.
